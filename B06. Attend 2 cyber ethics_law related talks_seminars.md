# B6 - Attend 2 Cyber Ethics or Law Related Talks or Seminars

## Overview

This entry documents two cyber ethics or digital law related events. Both events connect cybersecurity with governance, ethics, risk, law, accountability, and responsible technology use.

---

## Event 1 - 23rd Edition of WLF GRC, Cyber-Security and Risk Summit 2026

### Event Title

Cyber-Security and Risk Summit 2026

### Event Focus

Ethics, ESG, corporate governance, cyber-security, and risk.

### Summary

This event included a special session focused on embedding ethics and ESG into corporate governance. The session discussed how organisations can strengthen accountability, transparency, and sustainability while managing cyber-security and risk.

The session highlighted that cyber-security is not only a technical issue. It is also connected to ethical leadership, responsible decision-making, regulatory expectations, organisational accountability, and long-term trust.

The speakers used practical strategies and real-world examples to show how leaders can include ethical and ESG considerations in governance processes.

### Key Topics Covered

- ethics in corporate governance;
- ESG and organisational responsibility;
- cyber-security risk management;
- accountability and transparency;
- sustainable leadership;
- responsible decision-making;
- governance, risk, and compliance.

### Special Appreciation Moment

The session also included a recognition moment where each panelist received a personalised memento award. This recognised their contributions to ethical leadership and ESG excellence.

This showed the importance of valuing leaders and experts who promote responsible governance and ethical organisational culture.

### What I Learned

I learned that cyber-security decisions should be guided by ethical principles as well as technical requirements. For example, an organisation may have the technical ability to collect or process large amounts of data, but it still needs to consider fairness, privacy, transparency, and accountability.

I also learned that ESG and cyber-security are connected. Poor cyber-security can harm customers, employees, investors, and society. Strong governance helps ensure that cyber risks are managed responsibly rather than treated only as an IT problem.

### Cyber Ethics and Law Relevance

This event is relevant to cyber ethics and law because it focused on responsible governance, risk management, and ethical leadership. These are important when organisations handle personal data, protect systems, comply with regulations, and make decisions that affect stakeholders.

## Event 2 - Monash Law Inaugural Annual Digital Law Symposium

### Event Title

Monash Law Hosts Inaugural Digital Law Symposium

### Event Location

Monash University Law Chambers.

### Event Focus

Digital law, technology regulation, legal challenges, and the impact of digital systems on society.

### Summary

Monash Law held its first Annual Digital Law Symposium, marking an important milestone in exploring the relationship between law and digital technology.

The event brought together leading experts, academics, and practitioners to discuss legal challenges and opportunities created by the digital age. The symposium focused on how law needs to respond to rapid technological change, including issues connected to digital platforms, data, privacy, artificial intelligence, cyber risks, and online governance.

### Key Topics Covered

- digital law;
- regulation of emerging technologies;
- privacy and data protection;
- legal challenges in the digital age;
- responsible use of digital systems;
- technology governance;
- rights, risks, and accountability in online environments.

### What I Learned

I learned that digital technology creates new legal questions that traditional law may not always answer easily. For example, digital platforms and AI systems can affect privacy, fairness, liability, intellectual property, and access to justice.

I also learned that law and cybersecurity are closely connected. Cybersecurity helps protect systems and data, while law sets expectations for responsible behaviour, compliance, accountability, and remedies when harm occurs.

The symposium showed that digital law requires cooperation between lawyers, technologists, academics, policymakers, and industry professionals.

### Cyber Ethics and Law Relevance

This event is relevant to cyber ethics and law because it directly addressed the intersection of legal systems and digital technology. It helped explain why cybersecurity professionals need to understand not only technical controls, but also legal responsibilities and ethical consequences.

## Overall Reflection

These two events helped me understand that cybersecurity is not only about technical protection. It also involves ethics, governance, law, risk, and social responsibility.

The WLF GRC, Cyber-Security and Risk Summit showed how ethical leadership and ESG can be embedded into corporate governance. This is important because organisations need to make responsible decisions about cyber risk, data protection, and stakeholder trust.

The Monash Law Annual Digital Law Symposium showed how digital technology creates new legal challenges and opportunities. It highlighted the need for law, policy, and cybersecurity to work together when addressing privacy, AI, data governance, and digital risk.

Together, the events showed that strong cybersecurity requires both technical controls and responsible decision-making. Cyber professionals should understand legal obligations, ethical principles, and governance expectations so that security decisions protect people as well as systems.
